Why Tmon uses this memory
=========================

Confirm these facts against the current checkout before changing anything:

- `Cell` is 24 bytes and carries character, foreground/background colors, attributes, underline color, state bits, and rare metadata identity (`crates/temon/src/grid.rs`).
- Live primary and alternate screens use dense `Vec<Vec<Cell>>` rows.
- Scrollback is `VecDeque<Vec<Cell>>`.
- `push_history` resizes every history row to the full terminal width before storing it (`crates/temon/src/grid/edit.rs`).
- At 120 columns and 10,000 history rows, dense cell storage alone is approximately `120 * 10,000 * 24` bytes, explaining the roughly 30 MiB payload.
- The parser’s transient buffers are not the steady-state memory problem.
- Existing public and internal consumers assume dense rows or `&[Cell]`, especially line traversal, snapshots, selection/search, persisted output, resize/reflow, and scrollback viewing.

Primary objective
=================

Reduce Tmon’s retained 10,000-row scrollback memory substantially while preserving exact behavior.

Acceptance targets, in order:

1. Required first milestone: reduce every 10K scrollback payload by at least 40% from the hosted Tmon baseline, with no workload exceeding 20 MiB final footprint on a comparable macOS run.
2. Preferred uncompressed target: approach Ghostty’s 11–13 MiB final footprint without adding a third-party dependency.
3. Stretch target: investigate compressed cold-history pages only after the compact uncompressed representation is correct and measured. Do not jump directly to a complex compression subsystem.
4. Keep empty and full-screen-only footprints effectively unchanged.
5. Keep Tmon’s parser/grid throughput and snapshot regression gates passing. A memory win that causes a material throughput regression is not complete.

Do not “fix” the benchmark
==========================

The following are prohibited:

- Do not reduce the 10,000-row workload, terminal dimensions, sample count, payload complexity, or semantic checks.
- Do not lower Tmon’s configured scrollback limit.
- Do not change `ri_phys_footprint` to RSS, allocator-request counts, static-size estimates, or another easier metric.
- Do not special-case benchmark strings or detect the benchmark process.
- Do not drop styles, underline colors, combining text, hyperlinks, wide-cell roles, wrapping, protection, alternate-screen behavior, reflow, search/selection content, or graphics anchoring.
- Do not add `unsafe` Rust or a production dependency to `crates/temon`.
- Do not weaken, delete, ignore, or rewrite failing tests to accept changed behavior.
- Do not cache a permanently decoded copy of compact history; that would hide the memory rather than remove it.
- Do not refactor unrelated PTY, parser, renderer, or desktop code.

Recommended implementation direction
====================================

Use a staged compact-history design. Keep live primary and alternate screens dense for parser and renderer speed. Compact a row only when it leaves the live primary screen and enters scrollback.

Start by introducing one explicit history-row representation owned by the grid, near the current `Grid`/`Cell` definitions. Choose its exact encoding after measuring realistic rows, but it must preserve the full logical row and all `Cell` semantics. A sensible first design to evaluate is:

- Store logical width separately.
- Omit or run-length encode trailing default cells instead of retaining 120 full `Cell` values.
- Consider a compact history cell using a small inline codepoint/state record plus row-local or page-local style IDs, with rare metadata retained out of line.
- Preserve a lossless conversion between compact history cells and public `Cell` values.
- Keep empty rows extremely cheap.
- Avoid one heap allocation per default cell or per ordinary character.
- Reuse row storage where practical, but do not retain dense capacity after encoding.

Do not assume trimming blank tails alone will reach the preferred target. Quantify it on all four benchmark workloads. If it misses badly, add a compact cell/style representation rather than stacking cosmetic container changes.

Introduce a narrow row-view/visitor abstraction rather than spreading `match Dense/Compact` through the codebase. It must support:

- Reading a logical cell by column, returning a default cell for omitted tail positions.
- Visiting all logical columns without allocating when the caller accepts a visitor.
- Materializing a temporary dense row only for legacy APIs that require `&[Cell]`.
- Decoding a history row back to a dense mutable row when resize/reflow or the previous-row wide-cell repair path genuinely needs mutation.
- Re-encoding immediately after such mutation; do not leave cold history dense.

Migrate the existing history read/write choke points instead of adding a parallel history path:

- History insertion and eviction in `crates/temon/src/grid/edit.rs`.
- `line`, `for_each_line_cell`, `for_each_line_cell_range`, viewport traversal, hyperlink lookup, and combining lookup in `crates/temon/src/grid/view.rs`.
- Public wrappers such as `snapshot`, `with_line_cells`, `for_each_line_cell_range`, selection/search, and persisted-buffer extraction in `crates/temon/src/lib.rs` and their callers.
- Resize and reflow paths that pull rows back from history or rebuild logical lines.
- History clearing and history-limit shrinking; memory must actually be released.

Correctness invariants and traps
================================

Preserve all of these explicitly:

1. Wide characters
   - Keep leading wide characters, tail spacers, and leading spacers coherent.
   - `clear_leading_wide_spacer_before` may mutate the previous row, including the newest history row.
   - One-column terminals and width-changing reflow must still make progress.

2. Combining text and rare metadata
   - Preserve inline and pooled combining text exactly.
   - Respect existing unique-owner assumptions and metadata IDs.
   - Do not duplicate or prematurely free pooled extras when encoding, decoding, evicting, or reflowing.

3. Hyperlinks and styles
   - Preserve OSC 8 identity, hyperlink presence, foreground/background/underline colors, all five underline styles, bold/dim/italic/inverse/hidden/strikethrough, and protected cells.
   - A visually blank but styled, protected, linked, or combining-bearing cell is not semantically default and cannot be trimmed as an empty tail.

4. Wrapping, tabs, and reflow
   - Preserve soft-wrap markers, tab cells, cursor continuation behavior, and logical line joining.
   - Resizing while scrolled and changing rows and columns together must remain differential with the pinned Alacritty behavior.

5. Scrollback and public reads
   - Preserve `history_size`, `display_offset`, line numbering, line bounds, viewport mapping, snapshots, search, selection, persisted text, and inspector state.
   - Traversal APIs must observe one coherent terminal lock/state.

6. Damage and rendering
   - Keep viewport damage and chronological scroll operations unchanged.
   - History compaction must not force full-frame damage during ordinary live scrolling.

7. Graphics anchors
   - Preserve line-based Kitty graphics placement through scroll, eviction, resize, reflow, clear, and alternate-screen transitions.

8. Memory release
   - Lowering the history limit and clearing scrollback must release compact storage and rare metadata.
   - Repeated fill/clear cycles must not grow retained memory without bound.

Required tests
==============

Add focused tests following existing test style. At minimum cover:

- Compact-row round trips for all-default, full-width non-default, short text with default tail, one-column, and maximum-width rows.
- Every `Cell` field and state bit survives encode/decode.
- Indexed/RGB foreground, background, and underline colors.
- Single/double/curly/dotted/dashed underline styles.
- Wide characters at the beginning, middle, and right edge; leading/tail spacer repair across a history boundary.
- Single and long combining sequences, both with and without hyperlinks.
- Protected and styled blank cells are not trimmed.
- History eviction frees associated rare metadata.
- Clear scrollback and shrink history release rows while keeping the terminal usable.
- Scrollback traversal, snapshots, search, selection, and persisted text return the same cells/text before and after compaction.
- Resize/reflow matrices while scrolled, including simultaneous row/column changes and a one-column destination.
- Primary/alternate screen transitions do not compact or expose the wrong screen.
- Randomized differential traces continue matching the existing Alacritty-backed fixtures.
- A regression test demonstrates that a 120x10,000 mostly plain history no longer stores 1.2 million dense 24-byte `Cell` values. Prefer a deterministic internal retained-byte/capacity inspection for this unit test; do not pretend it replaces the process-footprint benchmark.
